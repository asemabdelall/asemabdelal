## Intelligent Fitness & Nutrition

Rahafit transforms personal health tracking by combining cutting-edge artificial intelligence with an intuitive, bilingual experience. The platform automatically generates customized workout splits, calculates precise caloric requirements, and provides an on-demand AI coach to answer any fitness question.

## Adaptive Progression

As athletes progress, Rahafit automatically adjusts weights, repetition targets, and recovery periods, ensuring steady long-term results without burnout.
