The most successful mobile applications share a common trait: they feel like a natural extension of the user's hand.

### Design Principles
- **Thumb-Friendly Hit Zones**: Placing primary actions within comfortable reach on modern large smartphones.
- **Offline Confidence**: Ensuring users can continue browsing and working even when entering areas with low signal.
- **Micro-Delight**: Adding subtle haptic feedback and fluid animations that make interacting with the product satisfying.
